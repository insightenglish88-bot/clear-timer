2. Privacy Policy & Regulatory Compliance (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

2.1 Data Controller & Minimalist Collection

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and California Consumer Privacy Act (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100 et seq.), Clear Timer adheres to the principle of strict data minimization. We only process data required to provide application functionality:

  • Unauthenticated Users: Zero personal data is transmitted to our servers. Timer sessions, custom skins, classroom titles, and team times are held exclusively in your local browser storage (localStorage).
  • Google Authenticated Users: When you choose to authenticate via Google OAuth, we receive:
    • User ID (UID) provided by Google Firebase Authentication.
    • Email address (for account identification).
    • Display name and avatar URL (rendered client-side).
    • Classroom titles, team labels, and recorded completion times synchronized to your private cloud document.

2.2 Lawful Basis for Processing (GDPR Article 6)

We process personal data on the following bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): Explicit affirmative action when you click "Sign in with Google" to link your account.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Providing seamless real-time cloud synchronization of your timer data across your authorized devices.

2.3 CCPA/CPRA "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Info"

Absolute Non-Sale Guarantee: Clear Timer does not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal information or student classroom data with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or commercial entities under any circumstances.

2.4 Subprocessors & Cloud Infrastructure

We utilize the following vetted cloud infrastructure:

  • Google Cloud Platform / Firebase (Alphabet Inc.): Authentication (OAuth 2.0), Cloud Firestore (encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit via TLS 1.3), and Firebase Hosting CDN. Servers located in certified Google Cloud data centers adhering to SOC 1/2/3, ISO/IEC 27001, and EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

3. Children's & Student Privacy (COPPA & FERPA)

3.1 Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA, 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6508)

Clear Timer is designed to be safe for classroom usage with Young Learners (YLE) and K-12 students:

  • Students do not create accounts or provide personal data to Clear Timer.
  • Account creation and Google Authentication are strictly restricted to teachers, adult educators, and authorized facilitators aged 18 and older.
  • Educators are instructed to use generic group identifiers (e.g. "Team Alpha", "Group 1", "Yellow Table") rather than children's full names or personal contact information.

3.2 Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA, 34 CFR Part 99)

Clear Timer does not collect or maintain student education records, academic transcripts, behavioral logs, or disciplinary files. Team timing data recorded during classroom learning games does not constitute educational records under FERPA. Educational institutions maintain complete ownership and authority over classroom labels.

You have complete authority over your data. At any time, you can exercise the following rights:
